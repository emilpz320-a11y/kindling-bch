Kindling

All-or-nothing crowdfunding on Bitcoin Cash. Backers pledge BCH into a SolidCash contract. If the goal is met the creator claims it; if the deadline passes first, each backer takes their pledge back. Every action below builds and signs a real BCH transaction, checked by the BCH 2026 VM under mainnet size limits on an in-browser mock chain (no real funds).
